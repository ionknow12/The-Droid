A personal PDF manager for studying.

Inspector identifies a book by its contents rather than its filename, so renaming a
file, moving it to another folder, or reinstalling the app never separates a book from
your place in it. Your position is written whenever scrolling settles, so even a hard
kill cannot lose the page.

Shelves are built from the folders you already keep your PDFs in. Two books can be open
side by side. Margins can be cropped for scanned textbooks, night mode inverts lightness
while leaving diagrams readable, and the table of contents is one tap away.

Nothing leaves your device.
